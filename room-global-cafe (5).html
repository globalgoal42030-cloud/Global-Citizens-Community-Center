Inside the Global Cafe & Bake Sale

The Global Cafe is more than a gathering place — it's a fundraising engine for our global tour and a direct pipeline of support for No Kid Hungry. Buy baked goods, donate gift cards, or join the raffle. Every purchase comes with a free GCCC membership.

Want to run your own fundraiser? Here's what people actually earn.

With roughly $150 in starting costs, the national average Bake Sale for No Kid Hungry raises around $1,000 — a 6–7× return on your investment. Here's how the numbers break down:

Conservative: $300–$500 — from selling baked goods aloneAverage: $800–$1,200 — once community donations come inStrong: $1,500–$3,500+ — with social sharing, raffles & community promotionReal fundraisers have gone much further. One organizer started with a goal of just $100 and raised $1,800 at her first bake sale — then $3,400 at her second. Another raised $90,000 at a two-day community event. Every $10 you raise can connect a child with up to 100 meals. There is no registration fee and no minimum — just your community, your cause, and a table full of baked goods.
