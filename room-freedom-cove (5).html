What We Offer

Inside the Freedom Cove

Freedom Cove is where rights are understood, protected, and exercised. We believe an informed community is an empowered community — and that global citizenship begins with knowing your rights.

📜
Know Your Rights Trainings
Workshops on tenant rights, immigrant rights, labor rights, voting rights, and civil liberties — delivered in multiple languages.
🗳️
Civic Empowerment
Voter registration drives, election literacy, candidate forums, and tools to make civic participation accessible to all.
⚖️
Legal Literacy Workshops
Plain-language explanations of laws and legal processes that affect everyday community members — in partnership with Global Counsel.
✊
Advocacy Training
Learn how to organize, petition, lobby, and advocate for policy change at the local, national, and international level.
🌐
International Human Rights
Education on UN Human Rights frameworks, the Universal Declaration of Human Rights, and how to use international law as a tool.
🤝
Legal Aid Referrals
Connections to pro bono legal services, immigration attorneys, and community legal aid organizations worldwide.
