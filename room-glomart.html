What We Offer

Inside the GloMart — Global Market

GloMart is the GCCC's community economic engine. A marketplace where mission-driven businesses, artisans, service providers, and entrepreneurs from across the global community connect with buyers who share their values.

🛍️
Sell Your Products
List physical goods, digital products, handmade items, and community-made merchandise — reaching the entire GCCC membership base.
💼
Offer Your Services
Freelancers, consultants, tutors, and professionals can list their services and connect with clients inside the GCCC community.
🌱
Mission-Aligned Vetting
All GloMart vendors are reviewed for alignment with GCCC values and UN SDG commitments — community trust is our baseline.
🌍
Global Reach, Local Impact
Products and services ship, stream, or connect worldwide — while keeping economic value circulating within the community.
💳
Community Currency
GCCC members earn and spend community credits that unlock discounts, feature placements, and exclusive GloMart benefits.
📦
Storefront Setup Support
Need help setting up your store? The Business Lounge and Dev Dojo teams can help you build your GloMart presence.
