✉️ A note for grown-ups

Sign your Global Citizen up for the club!

GCCC Kids is a supervised, ad-free space built by parents and educators to make global citizenship playful. A parent or guardian creates and manages the account — kids just get to play, read, and earn badges.

  • Every activity is age-appropriate and supervised by our team
  • Free membership, always — funded by our grown-up community
  • Real-world missions kids can do at home or in the clubhouse
  • Partnered with No Kid Hungry, Save the Children, and Global Citizen
