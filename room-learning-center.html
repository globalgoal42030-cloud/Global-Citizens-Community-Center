What We Offer

Inside the Learning Center

The Global Scholars School inside the GCCC is designed for anyone who wants to grow. From K–12 enrichment to adult literacy and college prep, every program is built around the 17 UN Sustainable Development Goals.

📚
SDG-Aligned Curriculum
Every course and workshop connects to one or more of the 17 SDGs — making global learning locally relevant and actionable.
🗣️
ESL & Language Learning
English as a Second Language classes, language exchange pairs, and multilingual support for community members from all backgrounds.
👩‍🎓
Adult Education
GED prep, workforce readiness, digital literacy, and continuing education programs for adult learners at every stage.
🧒
Youth Enrichment
After-school tutoring, STEM workshops, arts programs, and mentorship for K–12 students in the community.
🏛️
College & Career Prep
SAT/ACT prep, college application guidance, scholarship searches, and career pathway counseling.
🎓
Intern & Volunteer Credit
Students can earn college credit by volunteering or interning with GCCC programs — apply through the dashboard.
