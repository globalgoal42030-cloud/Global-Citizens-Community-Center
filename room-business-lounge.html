What We Offer

Inside the Business Lounge

The Business Lounge is where global citizen entrepreneurs get down to business. From solopreneurs to scaling startups, we provide the tools, community, and connections to grow with purpose.

🖥️
Virtual Co-Working
Drop into our virtual co-working sessions, focus rooms, and accountability pods — work alongside fellow global citizen entrepreneurs.
💰
Funding Connections
Introductions to grants, pitch opportunities, impact investors, and community lending circles aligned with social good.
👥
Staffing Resources
Access the GCCC talent pool — connect with interns, volunteers, virtual assistants, and skilled professionals ready to support your mission.
📊
Business Tools Library
Templates, SOPs, pitch decks, financial models, and operational frameworks — all free for GCCC members.
🤝
Operations Support
One-on-one advisory sessions with business coaches, legal guidance via Global Counsel, and peer mentorship.
🚀
Start Your Fundraiser
Need emergency funding? Launch a fundraiser for personal or business needs through our OneCause platform.
