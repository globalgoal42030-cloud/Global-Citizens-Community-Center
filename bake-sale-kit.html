No-Bake, Vegan, Gluten-Free Bake Sale Kit

7 Continents · 7 Days · 17 Teams · In partnership with No Kid Hungry and the World's Greatest Race — fueling our SDG tour, one bake sale at a time.

How It Works

Run Your Own Bake Sale — No Oven Required

Every dollar you raise helps send one of 17 teams around the world, and every recipe on this list skips the oven, dairy, and gluten — so anyone can bake, and anyone can eat.

1️⃣
Pick Your Recipes
No-bake energy bites, oat bars, and fruit-sweetened treats — all vegan and gluten-free. See the starter list below.
2️⃣
Set a Date & Spot
Your porch, your office breakroom, a school pickup line, or fully virtual with delivery/pickup by donation.
3️⃣
Register Your Sale
Create your personal fundraising page so every dollar is tracked toward the $2M team goal.
4️⃣
Spread the Word
Share your page link, print a flyer, and post your table — every share brings more bakers in.
5️⃣
Get Your Kit
Checklists, an event guide, a business-outreach script, sponsor stickers, and everything else you need — all in one place.
