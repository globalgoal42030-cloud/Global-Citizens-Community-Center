Which Office Is Right For You?

Public office isn't just Congress or the Presidency — most first-time candidates start local. Here's the general ladder of leadership roles that exist in most countries, from neighborhood-level to international.

🏘️
Local & Municipal
School boards, city councils, town boards, mayors, and other community-level positions — the most common entry point, often nonpartisan and lower-cost to run for.
🏛️
Regional & Provincial
County commissioners, state or provincial legislatures, governors and premiers — roles that set policy across a wider region.
🏢
National Legislature
Parliament, congress, or national assembly seats — where national laws and budgets are made.
🇺🇳
Head of State or Government
President, prime minister, or equivalent — the highest elected office in a country, with the steepest eligibility requirements.
⚖️
Judicial & Appointed Roles
In many places, judges, sheriffs, and other officials are elected too — often nonpartisan races with their own rules.
🌍
International & Youth Bodies
UN Youth Delegate programs, model UN leadership, and international advocacy bodies — a way to build civic leadership experience before running for office at home.
