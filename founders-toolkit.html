Intellectual Property Protection

Protect your name, your original content, and your ideas early — before a competitor, a bad-faith partner, or a simple oversight costs you the rights to them.

  • Know that copyright protection is automatic the moment you create original work — no filing required to have basic rights
  • Register your most important works with the U.S. Copyright Office at copyright.gov to unlock the right to sue for infringement
  • Search uspto.gov before settling on your name or logo, then file a trademark once you're using it in commerce
