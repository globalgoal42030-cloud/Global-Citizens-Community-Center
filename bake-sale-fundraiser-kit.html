🤝 Business & Community Outreach Script

Use these scripts when approaching businesses, organizations, and community groups to co-host a bake sale fundraiser with GCCC and No Kid Hungry. Adapt for in-person, email, phone, or social media DM. The goal: get them to register at bakesale.nokidhungry.org and co-brand with Global Citizens Community Center.

IN-PERSON / COLD WALK-IN

Script A — Talking to a Business Owner or Manager

Best for: cafés, hair salons, boutiques, barbershops, small restaurants, community centers
Hi! My name is [Your Name] and I'm with the Global Citizens Community Center — we're a nonprofit here in the community working with No Kid Hungry to fight childhood hunger. We're organizing a bake sale fundraiser and we're looking for local businesses and community partners to co-host with us. It's completely free to participate — No Kid Hungry gives every host a free fundraiser kit, their own donation page, and all the tools to run it. We handle the branding, the baked goods, and the social media promotion. Here's how it works for your business: We'd set up a bake sale table outside or inside your space — you get named as a community partner on all our flyers, our social media, and our website. All proceeds go directly to No Kid Hungry, which provides meals for children right here in America. We have two options — a classic bake sale or a fully vegan and gluten-free one, so we can fit any neighborhood or customer base. Would you be open to hosting us for a day? Registration literally takes 5 minutes at bakesale.nokidhungry.org and it's free. We'd love to have [Business Name] as a partner.
Tip: Have your flyer printed (from your HP printer) and hand it over. Ask for the owner or manager by name if they aren't available — leave a flyer and follow up within 48 hours.
PHONE / VOICEMAIL

Script B — Calling an Organization or Business

Best for: churches, mosques, temples, community organizations, schools, gyms, nonprofits
Hi, my name is [Your Name] calling from the Global Citizens Community Center. We're a local nonprofit partnering with No Kid Hungry for a community bake sale fundraiser. I'm reaching out because we're looking for organizations to partner with us — it's completely free and your team can run it at your location on your own schedule. Every dollar raised provides ten meals for a child in need. We'd love to have [Organization Name] as an official community partner. I can send you our full kit information and it only takes 5 minutes to register. Could I speak with someone about that, or is there an email I can send our partnership info to? My contact is [Your Name] at [email/phone]. I'll also send a follow-up. Thank you so much — have a great day!
Tip: Always follow up the same day by email or text. Attach the outreach flyer (print from kit, scan to PDF, or email as attachment). Mention that this is a time-sensitive 2026 campaign.
EMAIL OUTREACH

📖 GCCC × No Kid Hungry Bake Sale Host Guide

This guide is built from official No Kid Hungry bake sale resources at bakesale.nokidhungry.org and adapted for GCCC events. Print this tab or save it as a PDF to include in every kit.

🟠

The mission: 1 in 7 kids in America live with hunger. Every $10 you raise can connect a child with up to 100 meals. Every cookie and cupcake can make a difference. Register free at bakesale.nokidhungry.org — no fee, no minimum.

📅 Step 1 — Plan Your Event (2–3 Weeks Before)

REGISTER FIRST

Sign Up at bakesale.nokidhungry.org

Free · Takes 5 minutes · No fundraising minimum required
There is no registration fee to host a bake sale, and no fundraising minimum is required. Once registered, you'll receive your own fundraising webpage and a free toolkit including guides, posters, flyers, and table decorations to help you plan and promote your event. After registering: • Set a visible fundraising goal — No Kid Hungry recommends picking a goal you feel you can meet and adjusting it along the way • Share your personal fundraising page link via email, text, and social media right away • Download and print your free toolkit from bakesale.nokidhungry.org/resources
CHOOSE YOUR LOCATION

Find the Right Spot

Bake sales can be hosted any time of the year. Simply find a local business, school, place of worship, community center, or neighborhood that will allow you to hold your bake sale. Timing and visibility matter. Choose a date at least 2–3 weeks out to give your team time to plan. Look for built-in audiences where people are already gathering and might be hungry or open to a sweet impulse buy. Best locations for GCCC bake sales: • Outside a partner business (NYPD precinct, NAN office, church entrance, community center lobby) • Farmers markets, weekend block parties, school events • Inside your GCCC location during an open event or meeting • Using a visible location that gets solid foot traffic is most important.
BUILD YOUR TEAM

Recruit Bakers & Volunteers

Gather your friends, family, or coworkers and your favorite recipes, and start baking. A bake sale is a perfect opportunity to teach young people the importance of giving back to the community. Every bake sale needs: • 2–3 bakers (classic or vegan GF — see your checklist tab) • 2 table staff (one to sell, one to manage donations & QR code) • 1 social media person to post live on TikTok and Instagram • Optional: 1 sign maker / decorator for the festive setup Assign shifts if the event runs multiple hours. Use a simple group chat to coordinate.

📣 Step 2 — Promote Before Your Event

SOCIAL MEDIA

Post, Share & Go Live

When you host a Virtual Bake Sale component, it may be as simple as posting the link to your fundraising webpage on social media or sending it out in emails asking your friends and family to make online donations. Connecting your bake sale fundraising page to a Facebook fundraiser is simple and will guarantee more donations on that platform. You can also livestream your favorite content to help raise money for hungry kids — share your expertise and make a huge difference. Simply create your fundraising campaign, set your goal, and get ready to start streaming. GCCC Social Checklist: □ Post a "countdown" story 1 week before on TikTok + Instagram □ Share your No Kid Hungry fundraising page link in bio □ Film a baking prep video the night before — post it morning of event □ Go LIVE on TikTok during the event — film the table setup, moss water display, customers □ Use hashtags: #NoKidHungry #GCCC #GlobalCitizensCommunityCenter #BakeSale #VeganBakeSale □ Tag all GCCC partners in your posts
No Kid Hungry tip: You can also do online activities like live baking classes to help your fundraiser get noticed. Consider going live while prepping your moss water or cookies the evening before!
PRINT & HANG

Posters, Flyers & Signs

Download and print the posters and flyers from bakesale.nokidhungry.org/resources to hang around your community and spread the word. No Kid Hungry also provides table decorations and signage you can print to make your bake sale look more festive. Print from your HP printer (see Printing Setup in your checklist): • 2× vinyl banners (ordered from BannersOnTheCheap or Vistaprint) • No Kid Hungry posters — download free from bakesale.nokidhungry.org/resources • Your custom QR code from your fundraising page • Price tags, recipe cards, allergy info cards (all templates in kit) • GCCC × No Kid Hungry × Nobel Prize sticker sheets (see Stickers tab) • AI Local TikTok sponsor stickers (see Stickers tab)

🎪 Step 3 — Event Day Setup & Selling Tips

SETUP · ARRIVE 30 MINS EARLY

Table Setup in Order

1. Place 6 ft folding table in your chosen high-traffic spot 2. Set up 2 chairs behind the table 3. Drape tablecloth (white or branded) 4. Hang both vinyl banners — front of table or nearby surface with zip ties 5. Place QR code printout at eye level — customers can scan to donate even without cash 6. Set donation jar front and center, clearly labeled 7. Arrange baked goods by type — group cookies, brownies, bread, drinks separately 8. Place price tags and allergy cards in front of every item 9. SELLING TIPS

How to Maximize Sales at the Table

Offering a few surprises can really boost sales — think items with edible glitter, individually wrapped cake pops, or allergy-friendly treats like gluten-free options. Pricing strategy: • Price each item at least 3× the ingredient cost for a healthy profit margin • Suggested pricing: $2–5 per item, or bundle 3 for $10 • Lemonade & moss water: $2–3/cup, or "Refresh Duo" bundle for $4 • Bread loaf: $4–5/loaf or $2/half Selling tips that work: • Greet every person who walks by — a warm hello converts browsers to buyers • Tell your cause in one sentence: "We're raising money to feed hungry kids — every $1 = 10 meals!" • Offer samples of cookies or brownies — taste closes the sale • Point out the QR code for anyone who says they don't have cash • Vegan + GF items: lead with "it tastes amazing AND it's fully vegan and gluten-free" — curiosity sells • The moss water display is your TikTok hook — have it visible and gorgeous
